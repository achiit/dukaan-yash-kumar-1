About us

A village business that grew one function at a time

My name is Yash Kumar, and I started this work in Babhandih more than five years ago. I first learned the basics by helping my chacha set up flowers for weddings in our area, carrying marigold garlands at dawn before the baraat came.

Those mornings taught me that decoration is not just about looking rich. It is about a family standing in front of the stage and feeling that their function is special. That feeling is what I try to give every customer, whether the booking is a ₹500 bouquet or a full wedding.

Today we handle weddings, birthdays, ring ceremonies and house warmings across Barwadih and Latehar. Call me directly and we will plan it together.
